## P1 issues (5)

### --v4-ink-3 text fails WCAG AA (system-wide)
#9a9a9a on white = 2.83:1, floor is 4.5:1. Used in every eyebrow label and secondary text across the app. Highest-leverage fix in the audit.
Fix: darken --v4-ink-3 to ~#6f6f6f.
Suggested: /impeccable polish ink-3-contrast

### Legacy palette still active
App.jsx ImportBanner, StudentCard, StudentTabs, SessionCalendar, ProgressBar, parts of HomeworkSheet/AssessmentTemplateDetail/StudentPage.
Suggested: /impeccable polish periphery-components

### 5 bg-black violations
Listed above.
Suggested: /impeccable polish black-purge

### 5 small hit-target buttons without focus-visible
CategoryPicker, AssessmentSelector, AssessmentForm cancel buttons; CalendarPage month nav.
Suggested: /impeccable harden close-button-primitive

### StudentPage EditModal missed
rounded-2xl + shadow-xl + bg-black/40 — same three issues as the WorkLightbox fix.
Suggested: /impeccable polish edit-modal
